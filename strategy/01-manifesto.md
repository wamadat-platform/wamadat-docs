# Wamadat — Product Manifesto

> *2026-06-08 — board-level orientation document. Read before any strategic decision. ~200 words.*

---

## What we are

Wamadat is the **infrastructure layer** beneath Arabic-language learning entrepreneurship.

Every Saudi academy, every serious Arabic-language instructor, every Gulf-region training organisation that wants to run a professional learning operation — should be able to do so on Wamadat without hiring an engineer.

Multi-tenancy, payments, audit trails, ZATCA invoicing, PDPL compliance, certification, attendance, live sessions, hybrid in-person flows — these are not features we sell. They are the **rail** we provide.

## What we are not

- **Not a course marketplace.** Coursera/Udemy own that game. We do not.
- **Not a generic LMS.** Moodle/Talent owns that game. We do not.
- **Not a video-with-quizzes tool.** Teachable owns that. We do not.
- **Not "Coursera in Arabic".** Edraak tried. Rwaq tried. The Arabic content gap is not solved by Anglo-cloning.

## What we promise

| To the academy owner | To the student |
|---|---|
| You own your brand, your content, your customer relationship, your data. | Your progress, certificates, and learning history are portable, verifiable, and yours forever. |
| Pricing is transparent. Wamadat takes a published transaction fee, nothing else. | What you learn here is verifiable by any third party via `/verify/{code}`. |
| You can leave with full data export within 30 days, no exceptions. | Your account, your data, your right to be forgotten — all one click away. |
| Compliance is built in: ZATCA Phase 2, PDPL, immutable audit log. | Your payments use Saudi-native rails (Tap, Tamara, Mada), never a foreign payment processor we hide behind. |

## How we operate

We move slower than venture-funded competitors. We commit to fewer features than market noise demands. We say *no* more often than *yes*.

What we ship, we ship to last.
