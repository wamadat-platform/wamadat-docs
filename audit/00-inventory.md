## What this inventory tells us

1. **Scope is real** — 283 PHP + 94 TSX is mid-size monolith territory. A genuine line-by-line audit by 8 humans would take 1–2 weeks. The reviewer agents below sampled aggressively and focused on highest-risk files.
2. **Test gap is the loudest signal** — 14 tests across 71 routes ≈ ~20% endpoint coverage. Critical revenue flows (checkout, refunds, gifts, consultations) need feature tests.
3. **Architecture is healthy** — 12 modules with consistent layering. Reviewers will mostly find nits, not foundational issues.
